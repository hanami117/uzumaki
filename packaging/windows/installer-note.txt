Uzumaki for Windows.

Please report what goes wrong at https://github.com/hanami117/uzumaki/issues

Good to know:

- Uzumaki is installed for you only. Setup never asks for administrator rights.

- The programs are not signed yet. Windows may have said "Windows protected your PC" when
  you started this Setup; it may say so again for a new version.

- You need an Apple Music subscription for Apple Music. Your own music files play without
  one. You sign in once, in a window of Apple's own web player.

- Closing Uzumaki's window can keep it playing in the notification area (next to the clock,
  maybe behind the ^ arrow) when "Close to tray" is on in Settings. Right-click its icon to
  quit.

- Your settings, your sign-in and the caches are kept in your user profile
  (%APPDATA%\Uzumaki and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Uzumaki). Uninstalling Uzumaki keeps them unless you
  say otherwise.
