## castLabs Electron (playback bridge)

https://github.com/castlabs/electron-releases (`v45.2.1+wvcus`), MIT, Copyright (c) 2017-2025 castLabs GmbH.
It runs as a separate process (the bridge in `bridge/`); Uzumaki talks to it over JSON on stdin/stdout and does
not link it. Chromium's own notices ship inside it (`LICENSES.chromium.html`). The Widevine CDM is not part of
Uzumaki or of its packages: Chromium downloads it from Google at run time.

The bridge's other npm packages (install tooling for Electron only): `@electron/get`,
`@electron-internal/extract-zip`, `debug`, `env-paths`, `ms`, `progress`, `undici`, `@types/node`,
`undici-types` (MIT); `graceful-fs`, `semver` (ISC); `sumchecker` (Apache-2.0).
